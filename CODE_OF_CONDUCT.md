# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in **DripCatch** (`raizestudio/dripcatch`) a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone—regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that foster an open, constructive, and respectful community for all contributors, maintainers, and users.

## Our Standards

### Encouraged Behavior

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming, clear, and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints, technical approaches, and levels of experience.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback during code reviews and issue discussions.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from the experience.
- Prioritizing what is best for the overall DripCatch community and user experience.

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or unwelcome personal attention in public or private channels.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory remarks, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome sexual advances.
- Publishing another person's private information (such as a physical address, email address, or personal financial data) without explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting spam, malicious links, or deceptive data to public issues or community catalog queues.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all DripCatch project spaces—including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, community catalog submissions, and official communications—as well as when an individual is officially representing the project in public or online forums.

## Reporting & Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the DripCatch team at **[contact@dripcatch.fr](mailto:contact@dripcatch.fr)**. *(For security vulnerabilities rather than conduct reports, please contact [security@dripcatch.fr](mailto:security@dripcatch.fr).)*

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and maintainers are obligated to protect the privacy and confidentiality of the reporter.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will apply proportionate consequences when addressing violations of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Correction:** For minor unprofessional language or isolated boundary missteps, a private written notice clarifying the issue and why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** For a single deliberate violation or repeated minor infractions, a formal warning with restrictions on interacting with the individuals involved for a defined period.
3. **Temporary Ban:** For serious or sustained inappropriate behavior, a temporary suspension from participating in or communicating within DripCatch community spaces.
4. **Permanent Ban:** For a pattern of violations, targeted harassment, or severe misconduct, a permanent ban from all public interaction within the DripCatch project and community.
